Campaign reports usually die in their format: a 40-page PDF, average numbers, and recommendations nobody reads. What actually gets used in the weekly meeting is a single table.

## One table, four columns

| Channel | Cost | Opportunities | Cost per opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search | within budget | counted in the CRM | divided by two |

Those four columns cover 90% of the questions in the meeting. The rest get answered by opening the actual dashboard.

## Ground rules

1. Data is pulled on Monday morning, at the same hour every week.
2. No numbers without the date they were pulled.
3. If one channel cannot be measured, write "not yet measured" — do not leave it blank.

An empty cell raises questions; "not yet measured" raises action. That small difference is what keeps a 30-minute meeting ending on time.
